About

Hello, I'm [your first name]

I run Aion Web Design from Wrexham, building websites for small local businesses.

Before web design, I spent around ten years working as a chef, including running a section and training junior chefs. I know how busy a small hospitality business gets, and how little time owners have for anything technical.

These days I work in tech retail, where most of my day is spent explaining computers to customers in plain English. That's the approach I bring to websites: no jargon, no pressure, and clear answers to your questions.

When you work with me, you deal with one person from start to finish. I'll listen to what your business needs, show you a first look before you commit, and be here afterwards when something needs changing.

Why "Aion"?

Aion is an ancient Greek word for a long age, or lasting time. A good website should be like that: built properly once, then looked after so it keeps working for years.
